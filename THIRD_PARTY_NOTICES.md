## Not redistributed

Card compilers, Python and libraries are fetched by Nix on first use into
the user's store and are not part of Tsugumi's distribution. Build tools
(CMake, Ninja, the Swift toolchain, tree-sitter CLI, erofs-utils) are used
only to build Tsugumi.
